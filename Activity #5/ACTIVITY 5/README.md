### Learning Reflection

**What Have I Learned?**

### **What Have I Learned?**

Through this activity, I learned how to use AI image generation and editing tools to create and enhance realistic images using simple text prompts. I also developed a better understanding of how artificial intelligence interprets instructions and transforms them into visual outputs. I realized that providing clear and specific prompts can greatly affect the quality and accuracy of the generated image.

One challenge I encountered was making sure that the edited image matched my instructions while maintaining the original image's quality and appearance. I addressed this by providing a more specific prompt to add a matching Golden Retriever plush toy beside the dog while keeping the lighting, shadows, and other details consistent.

In the future, I can apply what I learned by using AI image generation and editing tools for school projects, presentations, digital content, and other creative activities. I also hope to improve my skills by experimenting with different prompts and exploring other AI-powered digital tools.
